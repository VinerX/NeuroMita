## Product Map

- `Home` is the launcher-style dashboard for install, update, launch, and release feed.
- `Settings` is the full configuration surface for providers, characters, memory, media, language, and data.
- `Sandbox` is the operational dashboard for live feature status, quick toggles, and diagnostics.
- `Logs` shows the live tail of the application log.
- `Developer` is for advanced and contributor-oriented workflows.
- `Guide` is the in-app onboarding overlay; this wiki is the durable reference.
