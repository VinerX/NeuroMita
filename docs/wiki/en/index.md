## Product Map

- [Home](app:home) is the launcher-style dashboard for install, update, launch, and release feed.
- [Settings](app:settings) is the full configuration surface for providers, characters, memory, media, language, and data.
- [Sandbox](app:sandbox) is the operational dashboard for live feature status, quick toggles, and diagnostics.
- [Logs](app:logs) shows the live tail of the application log.
- [Developer](app:developer) is for advanced and contributor-oriented workflows.
- `Guide` is the in-app onboarding overlay; this wiki is the durable reference.
